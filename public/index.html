CODE REDTURBO SURGERY

An alien parasite. A speeding ambulance. Three players, each on their own screen. One very unlucky patient.

OR
PLAY AS:

P1 · DRIVER (LEFT)

  • WASD or arrow keys: gas, steer, brake
  • Shift boost. Beats the clock, but cuts your reaction time
  • Warning beacons light up hazards ~2s out. Shout callouts like "BUMP!" or "HARD LEFT!"
  • Potholes and oil jolt the surgeon and fling tools. Crashes hurt everyone.

P2 · SURGEON (CENTER)

  • Mouse moves the forceps
  • Hold LMB on a parasite to clamp, then drag away to pull. Keep tension in the green band
  • Hold RMB to brace against bumps (steadier, slower)
  • Avoid yellow nerves and the ribcage. Over-pulling tears tissue.

P3 · ANESTHESIOLOGIST (RIGHT)

  • Q sedative (BPM down), E adrenaline (BPM up). Hold the needle in the shifting green zone
  • R / Tab swings the surgical lamp on or off the chest
  • 123 or FGH swat flying tools in the left/center/right lane. Strike when the lane turns green
PHOTOPHOBIC
Burrows under the lamp. Lamp OFF to pull it, then switch the lamp straight back ON.
PULSE-LINKED
Throbs with the heart. Only comes out while BPM is in the green zone. Don't hit ribs.
ARMORED TICK
Long, heavy pull. Brace (RMB) on every bump or the forceps slip and slice.
WIN: reach the hospital with all 6 parasites extracted.   LOSE: patient flatlines or the 3:30 clock runs out.
